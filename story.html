Our Story

Not written for a day. Written for everything.

When I Lost Focus

There were days I drifted. You didn’t let me. You scolded me—not to control me, but because you believed in me.

When Time Was Against Me

You saved my time by stepping in. You helped with the kids. You created an environment where preparation was possible.

When I Needed Direction

You analyzed the market. You discussed roles and timing. You helped me prepare and negotiate when I felt unsure.

When I Broke

When I broke, you motivated me. When I was lost, you supported me. When I felt I was nothing, you spoke about me with pride.

When I Rose

You were the happiest person watching me rise. And the most tense when my work didn’t reflect my worth. That says everything.

Thank you for being my side — every single day.

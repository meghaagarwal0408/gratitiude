Megha & Ashish
A story of showing up, every day.

Our Story

Not about a moment in time or celebrating one day, but about the partnership that carried us through it all — the countless ordinary days that mattered most.

When I Lost Focus

There were days I drifted.
You didn’t let me.
You scolded me—not to control me,
but because you believed in me.

When Time Was Against Me

You saved my time by stepping in.
You helped with the kids.
You created an environment
where preparation was possible.

When I Broke

When I broke, you motivated me.
When I was lost, you supported me.
When I felt I was nothing,
you spoke about me with pride.

When I Needed Direction

You analyzed the market.
You discussed roles and timing.
You helped me prepare and negotiate
when I felt unsure.

When I Rose

You were the happiest person
watching me rise.
And the most tense
when my work didn’t reflect my worth.
That says everything.

Thank you for being my side — every single day, through the uncertainty, the effort, the growth, and everything in between.
